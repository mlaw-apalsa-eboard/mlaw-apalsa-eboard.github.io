Interested in learning more about studying at Michigan Law before heading to Ann Arbor this fall? Get in touch with our executive board at [apalsaeboard@umich.edu](mailto:apalsaeboard@umich.edu)!

Each year, APALSA hosts events during Michigan Law's Preview Weekends. We will be hosting multiple gatherings during the two Preview Weekends in 2026 for interested students to learn more about APALSA and life at Michigan Law. [Join our mailing list](https://tinyurl.com/apalsalist) for updates!
