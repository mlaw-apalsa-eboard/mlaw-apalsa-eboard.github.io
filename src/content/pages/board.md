---
title: The APALSA Executive Board, 2026–2027
description: Meet the 2026–2027 APALSA Executive Board at the University of Michigan Law School.
---

The APALSA Executive Board can be reached at [apalsaeboard@umich.edu](mailto:apalsaeboard@umich.edu). We hold elections twice a year—once at the beginning of the Fall Semester for our 1L Representative positions and once at the end of the Winter Semester for the rest of our board.
